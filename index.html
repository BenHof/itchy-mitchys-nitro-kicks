What is this game?

Itchy Mitchy's Nitro Kicks is a 3D car soccer game. You drive a rocket-powered car around an arena and try to smash a giant ball into the opponent's goal. You're on the blue team — score more goals than the red team before the 5-minute timer runs out.

Pick from 10 cars and 19 arenas, each with different physics. Drive up walls, dodge-flip through the air, grab boost pads, and blast the ball into the net.

Tips to Win

  • Grab boost pads — they're in the arena corners. You need boost to go fast and hit the ball hard.
  • Boost into the ball for a powerful shot on goal. Speed = power.
  • Double-jump + direction to dodge/flip. Great for quick touches and last-second saves.
  • Drive up the walls — the curved corners let you ride walls for aerial plays.
  • Defend your goal — the blue goal is on your side. Don't let red score!
  • Try different arenas — each one has different friction and bounce. Ice is slippery, cosmic void is bouncy, sand is slow.
