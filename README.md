# Itchy Mitchy's Nitro Kicks

A 3D car soccer game built with Three.js. Drive rocket-powered cars, smash a giant ball into the opponent's goal, and score in 19 wild arenas — from a Mars colony to a cosmic void to a candy land.

## How to Play

### Goal
Score more goals than the red team before time runs out. You're on **blue team**. The match lasts 5 minutes.

### Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| **W / S** | Drive forward / reverse |
| **A / D** | Steer left / right |
| **Space** | Jump (press twice for dodge/flip) |
| **Shift** | Boost (uses boost meter) |
| **X** | Powerslide |
| **C** | Toggle ball cam / car cam |
| **Q** (hold) | Car cam |
| **M** | Toggle sound |
| **R** | Reset car |

### Gameplay Tips

- **Boost pads** are in the corners of the arena. Drive over them to refill your boost meter.
- **Jump + jump** in a direction to dodge/flip — great for quick last-second touches on the ball.
- **Drive up the walls** — the curved corners let you ride the walls for aerial plays.
- **Hit the ball hard** — the faster you're going when you connect, the harder the ball flies.
- **Boost into the ball** for a powerful shot on goal.
- **Defend your goal** — the blue goal is on your side. Don't let the red team score.
